# DeliverUs – App Concept

## 🎯 Purpose
Help Catholics recognize, resist, and heal from early-stage spiritual oppression through daily prayer, sacraments, fasting guidance, and spiritual warfare strategies — in a gentle, accessible, and hope-filled way.

---

## 🧩 Core Features

1. **Daily Battle Plan**
   - Morning Offering and Protection Prayer prompts.
   - Midday Pause Prayer notification.
   - Evening Examination of Conscience and Renunciation Prayer.
   - Reminders to read daily 5–10 Gospel verses (start with Mark).

2. **3-Month Deliverance Program (Optional Track)**
   - Month 1: Healing & Cleansing
   - Month 2: Restoration & Rebuilding
   - Month 3: Strengthening & Mission Preparation
   - Progress bars and milestones.
   - Gentle reminders about fasting days, confession, house blessings, etc.

3. **7-Day Emergency Rescue Plan**
   - For users feeling heavy attacks (fear, sadness, despair).
   - 7-Day checklist: prayers, actions, media purification, confession prompts.
   - "If you fall" encouragement buttons: “Press here and pray instantly.”

4. **Battle Mode Activation (When Feeling Oppressed)**
   - One-button emergency plan:
     - Play Psalm 91 audio.
     - Display Emergency Prayers instantly.
     - Show "Jesus is Lord here" visual affirmation.
     - Option to bless yourself with virtual holy water ritual.

5. **Healing and Protection Library**
   - Prayers for: Marriage Healing, Family Protection, House Blessing, Self-Forgiveness, Spiritual Armor.
   - Daily Litany (e.g. Personal Spiritual Restoration Litany).

---

## 🎨 Visual & UX Style
- Calm, light, and beautiful: Colors like soft blue, white, gold accents.
- Sacramental feel: Images of crosses, holy water fonts, shields.
- Minimalist interface: Focus on prayer, calm, not distraction.
- Encouragements everywhere: Gentle messages like “You are a warrior of light” after prayer.

## 📍 Step 1: Visual Journey Map — User Flow for DeliverUs

1. **Home Screen**  
   - Greeting: ✝️ "Welcome, Warrior of Light."  
   - Buttons: Daily Battle Plan, Emergency Battle Mode, 7-Day Rescue Journey, Resources.

2. **Daily Battle Plan Screen**  
   - Morning Offering + Protection Prayer button.  
   - Midday Pause Prayer button.  
   - Evening Examination + Renunciation button.  
   - Toggle to mark completion.

3. **Emergency Battle Mode Screen**  
   - Auto-play Psalm 91 audio.  
   - Display Emergency Warfare Prayers.  
   - "Bless Yourself" virtual holy water action.

4. **7-Day Rescue Journey Screen**  
   - Steps Day 1–7 (Commitment→Celebration).  
   - Auto-unlock next step after completion.  
   - Encouraging messages after each day.

5. **Resources Screen**  
   - Prayer collections (Marriage Healing, House Blessing, Litany).  
   - Psalms (Psalm 27, Psalm 91), Gospel highlights.

6. **Settings Screen**  
   - Customize reminders.  
   - Notification preferences.  
   - Sponsor/donation links (optional).
